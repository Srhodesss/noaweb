Questions

When can I get one?

The private beta opens in Q2 2027 for a small group of teams and athletes.

What does it measure?

Brain activity (EEG), brain oxygenation (fNIRS), and heart rate and HRV (PPG).

Is it comfortable to wear?

It's designed to sit in/around the ear like an earbud. No straps, no headset.
