Privacy notice

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who we are

Noa is a student venture run by a team of engineers at Imperial College London. Noa is not yet a registered company. You can contact us at info@noawearable.com.

What we collect

When you register interest, we collect:

  • your email address
  • whether you are an athlete, a coach or both
  • how you found us, for example the link, ad or poster you used
  • the date and time you signed up, and that you agreed to receive updates

Why we collect it

We use it to send you occasional updates about Noa, to invite you to optional research or testing, and to count how many people are interested and where they heard about us. We only email you because you ticked the box to agree. We never sell your details.

Where it is stored

Your details are stored in a Google Sheet that only the Noa team can access. Our website is hosted by Vercel. We may later move the list to an email service to send updates, and we will update this notice if we do.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe, or until 31 August 2027 if the venture does not continue beyond university. After that we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details, or stop emailing you, at any time. Email info@noawearable.com and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Research surveys

Our optional survey is separate from this list. It has its own participant information and consent, which you will see before you start it.

Age

Registering interest is only for people aged 18 or over.
