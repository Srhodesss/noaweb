Two palettes, two type pairings and three wordmark routes for Noa. Use the switches at the top to see every combination across the whole page, including the three sample hero and data blocks. Pick one palette, one type pairing and one wordmark route.

Colour

Both palettes move the background off beige to a cool mineral grey and set text in graphite. The accent comes from the mood board's livery red and appears only on the primary button, live data points and small highlights. Contrast ratios are measured live against the page background (WCAG AA: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and graphics).

A Rosso

Truer racing red. Colder, more instrument.

B Ember

Orange-red, warmer. Closer to the current site.

The current background #f4f2ee and grey #9b978f (2.6:1, fails AA) are both retired. In both palettes the bright accent is never used for small text: it has a darker text shade, and the button fill is tuned so white button text passes AA.

Type

One grotesk for every word, with its own tabular numerals for scores and metrics only. Neither is a default system face. Both are self-hosted, so there are no third-party font requests.

Wordmark

Three lowercase routes, outlined as SVG in brand/wordmarks/. Each uses the accent once.

1 Full stop

Mona Sans Expanded with a red full stop. Declarative, like the copy: "ready." The dot doubles as the live indicator across the site. It's the extended sport-culture touch, kept to the wordmark.

2 Gauge

Schibsted Grotesk with the o redrawn as a readiness ring and a red reading. The most "instrument" route. The ring can become the score dial in the product UI.

3 Livery

Archivo Expanded Black, slanted like a car number, over a red livery bar. The loudest sport-culture route, straight from the mood board. Best on kit and merchandise. On the site it needs restraint around it.
